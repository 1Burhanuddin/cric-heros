tournament scoring app. This Privacy Policy explains what information we

Information we collect
-----------------------

### Account information

When you sign up, we collect:

*   **Name, email address, and profile photo** — via Google Sign-In, or the
    email and password you choose if you sign up directly.
*   **Phone number** — only if you sign in with a phone number (this method
    is currently not offered to new users, but existing phone-based accounts
    are still supported).
*   **Gender, playing role, batting style, and bowling style** — optional
    fields you can fill in on your player profile.

### Cricket data you create

To provide the App's core features, we store the teams, matches, tournaments,
and ball-by-ball scoring data you and other users create — including team
rosters, match results, player statistics (runs, wickets, catches, and
similar figures), and any photos you upload for a team or tournament (team
logo, tournament banner). This data is often visible to other users of the
App by design — for example, teammates, opponents, and tournament
participants can see match scorecards and player statistics, since that is
the purpose of the App.

### Automatically collected information

*   **Device and usage data** — device type, operating system version, app
    version, crash logs, and basic usage events, collected via Firebase
    Crashlytics to help us find and fix bugs.
*   **Push notification token** — a device identifier used by Firebase Cloud
    Messaging to deliver notifications about your matches and teams. You can
    disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not sell your personal
information to third parties.

How we use your information
----------------------------

We use the information above to:

*   Create and manage your account and player profile.
*   Let you create and join teams, score matches, and run tournaments.
*   Show you and other participants match results, scorecards, and stats.
*   Send you notifications about matches, teams, and tournaments you're part
    of.
*   Diagnose crashes and fix bugs.
*   Respond to support requests you send us.

Where your data is stored
--------------------------

Account data, cricket data, and uploaded images are stored with
**Supabase**, our database and backend hosting provider. Push notifications
are delivered via **Firebase Cloud Messaging**, and crash reports are
collected via **Firebase Crashlytics** (both Google services). These
providers act as our service providers — they process data on our behalf and
don't use it for their own purposes.

Your data may be stored and processed on servers located outside your own
country. We take reasonable steps to keep it secure wherever it's stored, but
no method of transmission or storage over the internet is 100% secure, and we
can't guarantee absolute security.

Sharing your information
--------------------------

*   **With other users:** teams, matches, and tournaments are inherently
    shared spaces — teammates, opponents, and tournament organizers can see
    the scoring data, rosters, and statistics tied to matches you play in.
*   **With service providers:** Supabase and Firebase (Google), as described
    above, strictly to provide the App's functionality.
*   **For legal reasons:** if required by law, to protect our legal rights,
    or to prevent fraud or abuse.
*   **We do not** sell your personal information, and we do not share it with
    third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Your choices and rights
-------------------------

*   **Edit your profile:** you can update your name, photo, and player
    details at any time from your profile screen.
*   **Delete your account:** you can permanently delete your account and
    associated profile data from within the App, in Profile settings. Cricket
    data you contributed to shared matches or teams (like ball-by-ball
    scores) may remain as part of the historical match record, since it's
    shared with other participants, but it will no longer be tied to your
    personal account information.
*   **Notifications:** you can turn push notifications off in your device's
    system settings at any time.
*   **Contact us:** for any other request — access, correction, or deletion
    of your data — email **[privacy@yourdomain.com]**.
